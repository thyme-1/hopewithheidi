About

Hi, I’m Heidi — and I’m so glad you’re here.

Support for heavy seasons

I’m a life coach who supports people navigating chronic pain, major life transitions, and seasons that feel heavier than expected. This work is deeply personal to me and shaped by lived experience, deep listening, and a hope-centered approach.

You don’t need to be fixed

I believe people don’t need to be fixed — they need to be heard, supported, and encouraged as they walk through their own journeys.

A safe, compassionate space

If you’re in a season where life looks different than you imagined, you’re not alone. I’m here to offer a safe, compassionate space to talk things through.

Services

Choose the level of support that feels most nourishing right now.

Free Consultation (10 min)

A gentle first conversation. Share what you’re navigating and we’ll see if we’re a good fit—no pressure, just clarity.

Best for: first-time clients

50-Minute One-on-One Coaching Session — $70

A 50-minute private coaching session for individuals navigating chronic pain, health challenges, or feeling overwhelmed by life. This is a supportive, non-judgmental space to be heard, encouraged, and gently supported forward.

Three-Session Package — $195

A discounted package of three 50-minute one-on-one coaching sessions. Ideal for clients who want continued support and time to explore goals, challenges, and next steps at a comfortable pace.

Four-Session Package — $260

A deeper support option with four 50-minute coaching sessions. Best for those seeking ongoing encouragement, accountability, and a steady, compassionate coaching relationship.

Coaching is for emotional support, mindset guidance, and encouragement only. This is not therapy, counseling, or medical advice.
